1) AB KYA NAYA HAI
------------------
employee khud absent hai ya phone par bata raha hai — ab MD/Admin uske naam
par application file kar sakta hai:

  /dashboard/leave/admin  ->  "📝 File a leave application for an employee"
  (tab kholne par wahi form aata hai jo employee ka hai)

- "Filing for" dropdown: "Myself — <aapka naam>" ya list me se koi bhi
  active employee. Employee chunne par request USI ke naam par banti hai.
- Leave Type, From/To date (back-date bhi), Reason — sab same.
- "Approve immediately" checkbox: tick karte hi application save + approve
  ek saath — us range ki Attendance "Leave" mark ho jaati hai (Present punch
  wale din, Holiday/Week Off chhod kar — wahi purana rule).
  Checkbox nahi tick kiya to request Pending rahegi aur normal approval
  queue me dikhegi (jahan se bhi approve ho sakti hai).
