2) DAILY DIGESTS AB SACH ME CHALTE HAIN (leave + overdue payables)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Pehle ye routes code me the par koi unhe roz call nahi karta tha — kyunki
vercel.json Hobby plan ke 2-cron cap par hai (teesri entry daalne se
saare future deploys block ho jaate hain).

Ab dono digest usi roz wale 03:00 UTC (08:30 IST) courier-poll cron ke
saath chalte hain (/api/cron/poll-fedex-tracking), poll khatam hone ke
BAAD, best-effort:
  • Leave digest  → "Aaj 4 log leave par — Neelu, Rahul…" + per-person
    range/type/company. Recipients: LEAVE_DIGEST_CHAT_ID (comma-separated)
    ya har active leave_admin jiska Telegram connected hai.
  • Overdue digest → "Overdue ₹X · 90+ ₹Y" + top 5 parties.
    Recipients: OVERDUE_DIGEST_CHAT_ID ya har Telegram-connected admin.
    Din me kuch overdue nahi to message skip (skipEmpty) — noise nahi.
Kisi bhi digest ki failure courier poll ko fail nahi karti; response me
`digests` object me status aata hai.

Agar aap baad me in routes ko alag scheduler se chalana chahein:
    GET /api/cron/leave-digest     Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET
    GET /api/cron/overdue-digest   Authorization: Bearer $CRON_SECRET
(?dry=1 = message + recipients dikhao, bhejo mat; ?skipEmpty=1 = khali din
chup raho.) Aur cron se double na jaye is liye env TRACKING_DIGESTS=off
kar dena.

Note: Telegram bhejne ke liye TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN pehle se hi chahiye
(attendance punch notifications isi se jaate hain).
