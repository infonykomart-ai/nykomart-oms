1) PROBLEM KYA THI (invoice 170 / SHARDA INTERNATIONAL RUGS)
------------------------------------------------------------
Aapne 6 items ki entry ki: 2970 (No GST) + 720 (No GST) + 3000 + 1200 + 1200 + 1200
  Entry screen ka total            = 10620.00
  Bill Payment / Party Ledger      = 10804.50
  Farak                            = 184.50 = bilkul 5% of (2970 + 720)

Wajah: purchase_bills.g_total_plus_gst (generated column) me "gst_rate_pct NULL
ho to flat 5% jod do" wala purana default hai. Lekin GST dropdown ka pehla option
hi "No GST" hai, aur wo NULL bhejta hai — is liye "No GST" wale bill bhi chupchap
5% ke sath Finance (bill_pass_register.total_amt) me chale jaate the. Document
Entry list / A4 sheet total_amount dikhati thi (is liye wahan sahi lagta tha),
jabki Bill Payment / Ledger / P&L stored total padhte hain (is liye wahan galat).


2) FIX KYA KIYA
---------------
- DB: "No GST" ab gst_rate_pct = 0 save hota hai (NULL nahi). 0 ka matlab 0% GST,
  is liye g_total_plus_gst = total_amount + round off — bilkul jo aapne entry me
  dekha tha. Generated column ko drop nahi kiya (warna P&L/report views ud jaate),
  sirf CHECK me 0 add kiya: 0, 2.5, 3, 4, 9.
- Purane galat bills bhi repair: created_at >= 2026-08-17 (GST picker wali date)
  aur gst_rate_pct NULL — unhe 0 kiya. Isse 170 jaisa har bill theek ho jayega.
  2026-08-17 se purane NULL bills ko chheda nahi (unka 5% historical default hai).
- bill_pass_register.total_amt sabhi purchase_bill rows ke liye
  purchase_bills.g_total_plus_gst se RE-SYNC kiya — Bill Payment / Party Ledger /
  P&L turant sahi ho jayenge. Payments / Credit-Debit note adjustments ko haath
  nahi lagaya. (Agar kisi bill ka inflated amount pe payment ho chuka hai to uska
  balance due thoda negative = overpayment dikhega, jo sach hai.)
- App: No GST par 0 hi save hota hai (save + edit dono). Naya shared file
  src/lib/purchase-bill-money.ts hi GST/total/label ka single source hai —
  Document Entry list, A4 view, bill statement, sab wahi use karte hain, is liye
  ab kisi bhi screen par total alag nahi ho sakta. (Bill statement me pehle per
  line 5% ka hardcoded fallback tha, wo bhi theek kiya.)


3) DOCUMENT ENTRY ME AB EK INVOICE = EK ENTRY
---------------------------------------------
- "Recent Purchase Bills" me ab HAR VENDOR INVOICE ka EK card aayega, item ke
  hisaab se 6 alag cards nahi. Card par: invoice no, "6 items · 1 invoice" badge,
  vendor, poora invoice total, invoice date.
- "▸ Items" dabane par usi card ke andar saari entries khulti hain — har entry ka
  description, size × qty @ rate, taxable + GST = amount, aur uske saamne apna
  Edit aur Delete button. (Storage waisa hi hai: har item ki apni row, apni
  qty/rate/GST — sirf dikhne me ek invoice.)

  print/PDF.

- Edit entries = items khol deta hai, jahan se har entry alag edit/modify ho sakti
  hai (vendor, invoice no, date, qty, size, rate, GST, description, round off).

SELECT vendor_invoice_no, gst_rate_pct, total_amount, g_total_plus_gst
  FROM purchase_bills WHERE vendor_invoice_no = '170'
 ORDER BY created_at;

-- Finance mirror wahi total dikha raha hai?

  FROM bill_pass_register
